SEARCH A LITTLE SMARTER

Better searches.
Better finds.

Use these tips to find useful information and browse more safely.

01 — Use specific words

Instead of "coding", search for "beginner Python tutorials".

02 — Search an exact phrase

Use quotation marks around a phrase, such as "solar system facts".

03 — Check your sources

Compare information from trustworthy websites and check when it was published.

04 — Protect your privacy

Never enter passwords, home addresses, or other private information into a search.

05 — Watch out for suspicious links

Be careful with unexpected downloads, pop-ups, and websites asking for personal information.
